## 📊 Sales & Marketing Dashboard (Excel + Power BI)

This project demonstrates end‑to‑end **data cleaning in Excel** and **interactive visualization in Power BI**.

### 🔧 Data Preparation
- Cleaned raw sales export (`Sales_Export_CLEANED.xlsx`).
- Removed **duplicate records** and handled **missing values**  
- Standardized inconsistent formats (dates, product names, city names, etc.)  
- Ensured accuracy for revenue calculations across multiple channels  

### 📈 Dashboard Features
- **Executive Overview** with total sales (₹224M) split by channel  
- **Category Analysis**: Furniture, Home Appliances, Clothing, Groceries, Stationery  
- **Monthly Trends**: Revenue breakdown by payment method and month  
- **Interactive Filters**: Year selection (2023, 2024, 2025) for dynamic reporting  
- **Visuals**: Pie charts, bar charts, and KPIs for quick insights  

### 🚀 Key Highlights
- Combines **Excel data cleaning** with **Power BI visualization**  
- Enables **interactive exploration** of sales performance  
- Provides actionable insights into **channels, categories, and seasonal trends**
